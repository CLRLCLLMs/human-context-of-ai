About the Social

This social event will explore the human and cultural context of Artificial Intelligence (AI), focusing on whose languages, values, and perspectives are represented in the AI systems we build and whose are still missing. The event will foster critical and interactive discussion around how AI understands people across different linguistic, cultural, and social contexts, and what can be missed when diverse perspectives are not adequately represented. Through facilitated discussions, interactive challenges, and community networking, participants will exchange experiences, question assumptions, and explore how different perspectives can shape the development of more inclusive and culturally grounded AI. Our goal is to bring together researchers, practitioners, students, and community members from diverse disciplines, regions, and backgrounds to build connections, broaden perspectives, and collectively reflect on whose voices should help shape the future of AI.

Who Should Attend

The Social is designed for the broader NeurIPS community rather than a specialised technical audience. Its focus on people, communication, language, and culture makes it accessible to researchers and practitioners across fields, as well as students and early-career researchers. We welcome attendees from diverse geographic, disciplinary, linguistic, cultural, and professional backgrounds, with most of the evening dedicated to meaningful conversation, shared experiences, and connections among participants.
